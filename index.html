About us

At LIFT, we are redefining campus convenience at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN). Between back-to-back lectures, lab sessions, and study groups, finding time to get a good meal shouldn't be a hassle. We use smart, autonomous drone technology to connect UNN students and staff with their favorite campus vendors and local restaurants. Whether you’re studying at Nnamdi Azikiwe Library, relaxing at Bello Hall, or hanging out at the Faculty of Engineering, we bypass campus traffic and long queues to deliver hot, fresh meals right to your designated drop zone in record time. Our mission is simple: save you time, fuel your hustle, and bring the future of delivery to the Den.
